Frequently Asked Questions (Real Answers)

How effective is the active noise cancellation on Sony WH-1000XM5?

The WH-1000XM5 uses dual processors (QN1 and V1) and 8 microphones with an Auto NC Optimizer. It delivers benchmark attenuation for low-frequency engine rumbles on flights and trains, as well as ambient office chatter.

Can Sony WH-1000XM5 connect to a phone and laptop simultaneously?

Yes. Multipoint Bluetooth connection is fully supported, allowing seamless audio switching between two paired devices such as a smartphone and a workstation.

Does the Sony WH-1000XM5 fold into a compact size like the XM4?

No. Unlike the older WH-1000XM4, the XM5 features a redesigned non-folding headband that swivels flat into its included carrying case rather than folding inward into a ball.

What is the battery backup on the Sony WH-1000XM5?

It delivers up to 30 hours of continuous wireless playback with active noise cancellation enabled, and up to 40 hours with ANC turned off. Rapid USB-PD charging provides 3 hours of playback from a 3-minute charge.
